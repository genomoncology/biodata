# Neutral types left in BioMCP

This review lists the public types in BioMCP's `biomcp-types` and `biomcp-adapters` crates that hold content BioData's boundary covers. It counts every other public type in those crates as BioMCP's own, by kind. It moves nothing and changes nothing in BioMCP.

## Sources read

- BioMCP: `origin/main` at commit `873f2fa62c5490fec01a38066b0177a8e00e5f45`, crates `crates/biomcp-types` and `crates/biomcp-adapters`. The local checkout was clean at that commit.
- BioData: `crates/biodata` at commit `859d653`, read with `git show`. The 0006 worktree's checkout sits at a later commit, so this review did not read its working files.
- Rules: BioData's Boundary section in `CLAUDE.md`, the 2026-11-01 ruling that BioData holds pure content, and BioMCP's 2026-10-23 decision that BioData holds neutral entity values, identifiers, coordinates, notation and vocabularies and `biomcp-types` holds BioMCP requests, replies, source identities and their schemas.

## How each type was filed

BioData holds pure content. A type is a candidate when it states an entity, an identifier, a coordinate, a notation, a vocabulary term, or a publisher's record of an entity, and carries none of the consumer concerns below.

A consumer concern is one of these:

- a request, a request echo or a source selector;
- a reply envelope, a fetch status or a reply status;
- a page, a cursor, an offset, a total, a capped list or a search aggregate;
- an index or a position;
- a snapshot, a fetch timestamp, or a database mapping.

"Status" here means fetch or reply status. A publisher's status for an entity, such as a recall status, a trial status or an HGNC gene status, is content. "Source identity" here means BioMCP's source registry, its snapshots and its fetch timestamps. A publisher's release version and a link to a publisher's record are content.

A type that adds one consumer concern to BioData content is a wrapper. It stays in BioMCP and should hold the BioData type and not copy its fields. This review counts wrappers as BioMCP-owned, in their own kind.

Every other BioMCP-owned type has one of these kinds:

- Request: a command, query, selector, filter, input or request echo.
- Reply: an envelope, card, page, section, result, status, health report, help text, error, freshness stamp, BioMCP-computed aggregate, or an adapter's reply to BioMCP. Adapter stream decoders that assemble a reply are filed here.
- Paging: an offset, total, window, position list, continuation or capped list.
- Source identity: a source enum, source or key declaration, component terms, a snapshot, or a source record reference.
- Raw source row: a row that carries a consumer concern in a publisher's wire shape, a deserialization row, an untyped cell map, or a row tied to an aggregator's record.

### Vocabularies outside the count

Some vocabularies in `biomcp-types` are written with BioData's `canonical_enum!`, `native_enum!` or `wire_enum!` macros, so the `pub enum` count below does not see them. A separate scan found 16 neutral vocabularies among them:

- `PublicationType` (`article.rs`), `CorrectionKind` and `CorrectionRelation` (`article/card.rs`), `CitationIntent` (`response/article.rs`) and `OpenAccessStatus` (`response/article/pdf.rs`).
- `AnnotationKind` (`response/article_sections.rs`), which names gene, variant, disease and chemical mentions. Its declared order is BioMCP's tie order, which a move would not keep.
- `CivicStatus`, `CivicKind` and `CivicSignificance` (`response/civic_vocabulary.rs`).
- `AssertionKind` (`response/variant_assertions.rs`), which names the VCV, RCV, SCV and expert-panel record scopes. `AssertionDomain` (same file), which names the germline, somatic clinical impact and oncogenicity classification domains.
- `OncokbGeneType` (`response/gene/oncokb.rs`) and `RnaUnit` (`response/gene/expression.rs`).
- `DrugRecallClassification` (`drug/recall.rs`).
- `NciTrialPhase` (`response/nci_phase.rs`) and `NciBiomarkerUse` (`query/trial/biomarker.rs`), which names inclusion and exclusion use of a trial biomarker.

`AlphaGenomeStrand` duplicates BioData's `Strand` in a publisher's spelling. This review checked `AlphaGenomeBiosampleType` and `AlphaGenomeOntologyType` and left them out. They describe the tracks of a prediction model's output, not a biomedical entity, and the ontology names they hold already appear among BioData's identifier namespaces. The scan did not classify the other macro-written types, most of which are section, selector and filter words.

## Types this review was unsure about

- `ArticleInput` and `ArticleKey` are identifier unions, and BioMCP also uses them as request inputs. This review files them as candidates by analogy with BioData's `AuthorId`. `CellLineInput`, `ProteinInput` and `GeneInput` are filed as requests, because they mix identifiers with lookup keys or free text.
- `PathwayHeader` is a projection of BioData's `Pathway` for search pages, like `GeneHeader`, but it carries no match or capped list, so it stays a candidate.
- `ClinGenValidity`, `GenccSubmission`, `Dosage` and the three OncoKB gene types are curated source statements. Their BioData module could be `disease`, `gene::annotations` or `variant::statement`.
- `CivicGene` and `DrugWhoCard` each carry one field to drop on a move: the connection total and the request echo. If the PM reads those fields as making them wrappers, the candidate count falls by two and the wrapper count rises by two.
- `CosmicCoordinates`, `AlphaGenomeVariant` and `AlphaGenomeInterval` hold coordinates in a publisher's convention. BioMCP would map them to BioData's checked types and not move them as they are.
- The six source timestamp types could also be read as neutral timestamp notation. This review files them as replies because BioMCP uses them only as reply freshness stamps.
- `DrugHeader` duplicates BioData's `DrugIdentity` but carries BioMCP's `DrugSource`, so it is filed as a reply. `DiseaseHeader` carries BioMCP's `DiseaseOntology` selector, so it is filed as a reply.
- `HotspotRecord` carries a membership match and is filed as a raw source row. `VariantComponent` holds a version and license next to BioMCP's component terms and is filed as source identity.
- The AlphaGenome stream decoders in `biomcp-adapters` (`Envelope`, `ScoreEnvelope` and `TensorStream`) are filed as replies because they assemble an adapter reply. They fit none of the kinds exactly.
